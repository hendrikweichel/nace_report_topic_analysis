## Overview

Efficient E-Solutions Berhad ('EFFICEN' or 'the Group') has more than 20 years in managing outsourced related services which includes document storage, scanning and Information Technology  ('IT')  services  for  financial  institutions.  In  2018, EFFICEN  expanded  its  IT  outsourcing  services  into  Cyber Security services by providing 24 x 7 x 365 days comprehensive managed security services to businesses. Document Storage segment  and  IT  services  segment  are  two  core  business segments  of  the  Group.  In  2022,  the  Group  incorporated  a subsidiary, Vigilant Asia Cybersecurity Pte Ltd in Singapore for provider cybersecurity services to Singapore market.

EFFICEN triggered  Paragraph  8.03A(2)  of  the  Main  Market Listing  Requirements  of  Bursa  Malaysia  Securities  Berhad ('Bursa  Securities')  on  31  December  2015  whereby  the Company has ceased its major business through the disposal of its document printing business and is required to submit a regularization plan to Bursa Securities.

EFFICEN had on 16 December 2022 applied for an extension of  time  to  submit  a  regularization  plan  to  Bursa  Securities. On 10 February 2023, Bursa Securities granted EFFICEN an extension of time up to 30 June 2023 to submit its regularization plan pursuant to paragraph 8.04(3) together with paragraph 5.0 of Practice Note 17 of the Main Market Listing Requirements of Bursa Securities.

EFFICEN has been evaluating several options to regularize its condition as an affected listed issuer pursuant to Paragraph 8.03A(3)(c) of the Listing  Requirements of Bursa Securities. On 22 February 2023, the Group entered into a conditional sale and purchase agreement ('SPA') with Datapos (M) Sdn Bhd ('Datapos')  for  the  proposed  acquisition  of  the  records management and storage services contracts and agreements between Datapos and its customers and some assets. This exercise will increase the revenue and customers base of the Group. The Group believes that it can provide more services to these customers, hence improving the financial performance of the Group.

On 28 February 2023, the Group had entered into a conditional share sales agreement ('SSA') with Lim Choon Chai and Lim Kui Tze for the acquisition of their entire equity interest held in  FCS  International  Sdn  Bhd  ('FCS').    FCS  is  principally engaged in the provision of scanning and digitizing hardware and solutions as well as information and content management solutions. The principal activities of FCS are complementary to the document archiving activities of the Group which is part of the document storage business segment. FCS would contribute positively and improve the Group financial performance.

## Prospects

Malaysia's economy  is  expected  to continue its growth trajectory in 2023, with a projected GDP growth rate of around 4.5%, as per Bank Negara Malaysia's forecast. This growth is  supported  by  ongoing  investments  in  infrastructure  and digitalization initiatives, as well as the recovery of global trade and demand.

As digitalization continues to accelerate, cybersecurity threats are expected to remain a significant concern for businesses in 2023. The rise of remote work and cloud-based services has expanded the attack surface, making it crucial for organizations to prioritize cybersecurity measures to protect their operations and data.

Financial  Service  Institutions  are  expected  to  continue  to increase  their  cybersecurity  spending  to  comply  with  Bank Negara  Malaysia's  guidelines,  and  small  and  medium-sized businesses are also expected to invest more in cybersecurity solutions  and  services  to  protect  themselves  from  cyber threats.

We  are  halfway  through  the  government's  Digital  Economy Blueprint 2021-2025 is expected to continue to drive growth in  the  digital  economy,  with  initiatives  aimed  at  enhancing cybersecurity measures and developing a stronger cybersecurity ecosystem. Furthermore, the government plans to  establish  the  Malaysian  Cyber  Security  Commission  as part  of  efforts  to  strengthen  cybersecurity  in  the  country,  as announced  by  Communications  and  Digital  Minister  Fahmi Fadzil on 31 Jan 2023. This will give greater emphasis of the importance of cybersecurity and its important role in keeping our country's digital journey on the fast track.

Our COO, Ramesh Naidu was awarded the prestigious award of  Cybersecurity  Professional  of  the  Year  by  Cybersecurity Malaysia  last  year.  This  award  is  a  great  recognition  of  Mr. Ramesh's outstanding contribution and leadership in the field of  cybersecurity.    Winning  this  award  not  only  highlights  Mr. Ramesh's personal achievements but also reflects positively on our organization and commitment to cybersecurity excellence.

For Vigilant Asia, there are opportunities to capitalize on the increasing demand for cybersecurity solutions and services by strengthening partnerships with technology partners, building awareness  of  services  and  solutions,  and  developing  new offerings to meet evolving cybersecurity needs.

The  document  storage  business  is  expected  to  continue  its steady  growth,  with  more  companies  expected  to  reduce office space and move documents to offsite storage solutions. Additionally,  there  is  an  increasing  demand  for  document storage solutions from hospitals and other healthcare providers.

Overall,  while  Malaysia's  economic  growth  is  expected  to continue in 2023, businesses must remain vigilant in protecting themselves  against  cybersecurity  threats  and  adapt  to  the changing  landscape  to  ensure  their  continued  growth  and success.

## Supply Chain Management

The  Group  as  one  of  the  leaders  in  Business  Process Outsourcing industry especially in Information Technology and Cybersecurity segment are always study the latest technology and solutions to meet dynamic business requirements and to fight  against  cyber-attacks.  We  evaluate  and  communicate with  the  industry  players  to  understand  the  latest  market trends and threats. We apply criteria and carry out the proof of concept and compare the outcome with different solutions before introducing them to our customers.  The Group put in place procurement selection criteria and policies to select the most suitable technology and suppliers which include but not limited  to  good  business  ethic  of  the  suppliers,  continuous improvement of the products and services.

## Data security and privacy

The Group strengthens its information technology by establishing  data  privacy  and  protection  policies  to  protect employees'  personal  data,  financial  information,  customers' data  and  other  sensitive  information.  The  Group  installed Endpoint  Detection  and  Response  ('EDR')  that  continually monitors an endpoint (e.g., computer, mobile phone, servers &amp; etc.) to mitigate malicious cyber threats. Upholding the Group's data security and privacy are always the upmost priority of the Group.

As  a  cybersecurity  service  provider,  the  Group  has  been actively conducting cybersecurity awareness to its stakeholders through training, email and posting in social media.

## Talent Attraction, Development &amp; Retention

The year of 2022 has been an exciting year as the country had reached a significant level of vaccination efforts against Covid-19, which has allowed for a return to a more normal way of  life.  Restrictions  on  travel  and  large  gatherings  had  since been lifted and many businesses had started to reopen at full capacity.

Seeing  the  country  rebounding  and  has  recovered  strongly from the Covid-19 disruptions of the past two years has given us hope that we can emerge from this crisis stronger and more resilient than before.

As  a  caring  organization,  we  understand  the  importance of  improving  talent  attraction,  development  and  retention to  rebuild  and  strengthen  our  workforce  post  Covid-19.    To achieve this, we will focus on the following in the coming year:-

## 1. Ensure clear communication

To  reduce  stress  and  build  trust  with  employees  who may have been uncertain about their job security and the Group's plans during the pandemic, we will prioritize clear and transparent communications.  This includes regular updates on the Group's plans and expectations to help employees feel engaged and informed.  Our goal is to ensure  that  everyone  can  effectively  contribute  to  the Group's success.

## 2. Prioritize employee well-being

We acknowledge  that  the  pandemic  could  have  had  a substantial  effect  on  the  well-being  of  our  employees, resulting in heightened levels of stress and anxiety.  To support our employees'  well-being,  we  can  provide them with resources and options such as mental health assistance, work  rearrangement,  and  creating  more opportunities for physical activity and social connection. We  have  taken  the  first  step  by  reopening  our  gym room and activated the  in-house sports facilities for all employees,  and  we  have  gradually  reintroduced  some social events after work hours.

## 3. Invest in employee development

Retaining skilled employees is crucial for maintaining a competent workforce, and one way to achieve this is by prioritizing  employee  development.    By  investing  in  the development of our staff, we can help them achieve their full potential, which can have significant benefits for the Group. To support the growth and career aspirations of our employees, the Group will continue to invest in various development  opportunities,  such  as  soft  skill  training, certification programs, workshops, and mentoring programs.

## 4. Create a positive workplace culture

We recognize that a favorable workplace culture can play a  significant  role  in  retaining  employees  and  fostering engagement  and  productivity.  To  establish  a  positive workplace  culture,  the  management  will  continue  to explore methods to encourage cooperation, acknowledge achievements,  provide  feedback,  and  demonstrate  a commitment to diversity, equity, and inclusion.

## 5. Offer competitive compensation and benefits

Continue  to  conduct  regular  market  survey/research  to ensure that our compensation and benefits are competitive and  offer  additional  incentives  such  as  healthcare  and insurance coverage as we believe employees are more likely to stay with an organization if they feel that they are fairly compensated and that their benefits package meets their needs.

## Employee Engagement &amp; Welfare

Employee  engagement  and  welfare  are  critical  components of  creating  a  positive  and  productive  workplace  culture.  We believe when employees feel engaged and appreciated, they are  more  likely  to  remain  committed  to  their  work,  be  more productive, and stay with the Group for a prolonged period.

Therefore, we are committed to continue enhancing employee engagement  by  creating an inclusive work environment where everyone is respected, valued and feels belonged.  We encourage  open  and  interactive  communication  at  all  levels to  enhance  work  processes  and  strengthen  bonding  among employees.

In  the  post-Covid  era,  we  acknowledge  that  the  health  and safety  of  our  employees  is  of  utmost  importance.    We  have implemented  several  measures  to  ensure  a  secure  working environment, such as providing personal protection equipment, routinely  sanitizing  the  workplace,  and  arranging  safety  and health training and awareness programs for employees at all levels.

Moreover,  we  understand  the  need  for  flexibility  in  the  new normal, and we are committed to providing work re-arrangement that cater to the evolving needs and family obligations of our employees.

By  prioritizing  employee  engagement  and  welfare,  we  aim to  establish  a  workplace  culture  that  nurtures  innovation, collaboration,  and  a  sense  of  purpose,  leading  to  long-term business sustainability and success.

## STATEMENT ON RISK MANAGEMENT AND INTERNAL CONTROL

## INTRODUCTION

The  Board  of  Directors  ('Board')  of  Efficient  E-Solutions Berhad  ('Efficient'  or  'the  Company')  is  pleased  to  present its Statement on Risk Management and Internal Control ('the Statement') for the financial year ended 31 December 2022. This Statement describing the state of risk management and internal  control  of  the  Company  and  its  subsidiaries  ('the Group')  is  prepared  and  disclosed  pursuant  to  paragraph 15.26(b)  and  Practice  Note  9  of  the  Main  Market  Listing Requirements ('MMLR') of Bursa Malaysia Securities Berhad ('Bursa Securities') and is guided by the Statement on Risk Management  &amp;  Internal  Control:  Guidelines  for  Directors  of Listed Issuers ('the Guidelines') and the Malaysian Code on Corporate Governance 2021 ('MCCG').

## BOARD RESPONSIBILITIES

The Board acknowledges its overall responsibility for reviewing the  adequacy and integrity  of  the  Group's  risk  management and  internal  control  systems,  identifying  principal  risks  and establishing appropriate controls and frameworks to manage risks  to  safeguard  shareholders'  interests  and  the  Group's assets.

The  Board  understands  the  principal  risks  of  the  business that  the  Group  involves.  The  Board  also  recognised  that business decisions require the balancing of risk and return to reward the shareholders. Functionally, risk management is the responsibility of all Executive Directors and management staff members who manage the business risks in the Group and ensure that businesses are under control.

## RISK MANAGEMENT

The Board has put in place a Risk Management Policy as the governance  structure  and  processes  for  risk  management on enterprise wide, in order to embed the risk management practice into all level of the Group and to manage key business risks  faced  by  the  Group  as  well  as  to  continuously  explore new business opportunities available to the Group adequately and effectively.

The  Company  remains  as  the Affected  Listed  Issuer  under Paragraph  8.03A  of  the  MMLR.  Therefore,  the  Board  main focus  is  to  continuously  explore  new  business  opportunities to invest in and acquire in order to generate and maintain an adequate  level  of  operation  to  warrant  continued  trading  or listing on Bursa Securities.

While identifying suitable investment options, the Board and management  continue  pooling  their  effort  in  studying  and evaluating new business opportunities and when appropriate, may engage professionals to conduct due diligence on these targeted businesses to be acquired. During the due diligence process,  the  Board  assesses  the  viability,  profitability,  and growth potential of the identified  target  business,  as  well  as considers  the  alignment  of  these  business  options  with  the Group's risk appetite.

The  outcome  and  progress  of  the  investment  evaluations and due diligence are briefed by the Executive Directors and management to the Board for discussion and decision making. At this stage, the Independent Directors' views and opinions on the targeted businesses were provided to ensure that business decisions are made objectively and soundly.

As a result of the prolonged pandemic, the Group  is experiencing some delay in its exploration of suitable business investment option under its regularisation plan. The Company has applied to Bursa Securities and was granted an extension of time up to 30 June 2023 to submit its regularisation plan.

- (ii) Management organisation structure defining the management responsibilities and hierarchical structure of monitoring, reporting and accountability;
- (iii) Management  monitoring  and  review  of  financial  and operational performance of the present Group's activities;
- (iv) The Audit Committee ('AC') reviews in consultation with management,  the  unaudited  quarterly  financial  results and the Group's progress towards achieving the desired financial performance;
- (v) An internal audit function to assist the AC and the Board in conducting independent assessment on the systems of internal control and the governance practices;
- (vi) The  AC  overseeing  management  implementation  of remedial  action  for  findings  identified  by  the  Internal Auditors;
- (vii)  Board representations in the company which the Group has  investment  namely  Regalia  Records  Management Sdn Bhd ('RRM');
- (viii) The internal and  external physical security controls installed in the premises to prevent unauthorized access of the building and customers' details and information.

## Revenue from contracts with customers (cont'd)

Revenue is measured at the amount of consideration to which the Group expects to be entitled in exchange for transferring the promised goods or services to the customers, excluding amounts collected on behalf of third parties such as sales taxes or services taxes. - a)    the customer simultaneously receives and consumes the benefits provided by the Group's performance as the Group performs;
- b)    the Group's performance creates or enhances an asset that the customer controls as the asset is created or enhanced; or
- c) the Group's performance does not create an asset with an alternative use and the Group has an enforceable right to payment for performance completed to date.

Revenue for performance obligation that is not satisfied over time is recognised at the point in time at which the customer obtains control of the promised goods or services.

Revenue is recognised only when it is probable that the Group will collect the consideration to which it will be entitled to in exchange for goods or services sold.

Deferred tax is measured at the tax rates that are expected to apply in the reporting period when the asset is realised or the liability settled, based on tax rates that have been enacted or substantively enacted at the reporting date. Deferred tax is recognised in profit or loss.

Deferred tax assets and liabilities are offset if there is legally enforceable right to offset current tax liabilities and assets, and they relate to income taxes levied by the same tax authority on the same taxable entity.

## Impairment of assets

The carrying amount of assets subject to accounting for impairment (primarily non financial assets) is reviewed at each reporting date to determine whether there is any indication of impairment. If any such indication exists, the asset's recoverable amount is estimated. An impairment loss is recognised whenever the carrying amount of an asset or the cash generating unit to which it belongs exceeds its recoverable amount. Impairment losses are recognised in profit or loss in the reporting period in which it arises.

The recoverable amount is the greater of the asset's net selling price and its value in use. In assessing value in use, estimated future cash flows are discounted to their present value using a pre-tax discount rate that reflects current market assessments of the time value of money and the risks specific to the asset. For an asset that does not generate largely independent cash inflows, the recoverable amount is determined for the cash generating unit to which the asset belongs.

An impairment loss in respect of goodwill is not reversed. In respect of other assets, an impairment loss is reversed if there has been a change in the estimates used to determine the recoverable amount. An impairment loss is reversed only to the extent that the asset's carrying amount does not exceed the carrying amount that would have been determined, net of depreciation or amortisation, if no impairment loss had been recognised. The reversal is recognised in profit or loss.

## Expected credit losses (cont'd)

ECL are recognised in two stages.
