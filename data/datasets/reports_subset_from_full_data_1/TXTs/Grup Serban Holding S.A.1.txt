## BRIEF HISTORY

Any discrepancy between the Romanian and English versions will consider the Romanian version as Grup Șerban Holding ("the Issuer" or "GSH") was established on October 16, 2018, as a limited liability company, and on December 5, 2019, it was transformed into a joint-stock company, obtaining the ONRC resolution on December 16, 2019. The formation of this company was driven by the need to have a parent company to control the other companies with distinct activities, components of the Șerban group of companies, in light of the accelerated growth plans, specifically: INTERAGROALIMENT S.R.L. (IAA), established in 1994, registered with the Trade Register 33.8 million lei under no. J04/850/1994, CUI RO5845723; PROFIT NET FERMA AVICOLĂ ȘERBAN S.R.L. (FAS), renamed in 2008, and established in the year 2000 under the name Rom Trading Product S.R.L, registered with the Trade Register under +31% vs. 2021 no. J04/1787/2008, CUI RO12977123; FABRICA DE PÂINE ȘERBAN S.R.L. (FPS), established in 2008, registered with the Trade Register under no. J04/1211/2008, CUI RO24124510; ȘERBAN DISTRIBUȚIE S.R.L. (SD), established in 2005, registered with the Trade Register under no. J04/1965/2005, CUI RO18096106. As of the date of this report, the Issuer is the sole shareholder of the companies mentioned above. GSH represents a group of Romanian capital companies, currently in full expansion, which began its operations in Onești in 1994. The group's structure is integrated and includes the following fields of activity: agriculture, grain trade, oilseeds and vegetables, poultry farming, Angus cattle farming, bakery, pastry-confectionery, public food services (its own chain of stores - Băcăniile Șerban and a cafeteria under the LuMa flavor creator brand), distribution, and transportation.

<!-- image -->

In all its fields of activity, the Issuer plans new projects, seizes new opportunities, and consolidates its current business, all of which can only be achieved with the full involvement of human resources.The

Issuer's mission is to provide its partners with high-quality products and services, create an environment where employees can fully realize their potential, and develop a trustworthy brand for Romania and beyond. GSH is a dynamic company that continuously supports and promotes a healthy lifestyle through the products it offers, understanding that societal impact must be accompanied by financial resilience and performance. GSH's vision is to become the first choice of local customers for food products (poultry, bakery, pastry, and confectionery) and to set trends in the local specialty market, with the Issuer aiming to become a top producer in Romania.

## DESCRIPTION OF THE ISSUER'S ACTIVITY

The activity of GSH began in 1994 when INTERAGROALIMENT S.R.L. focused on the production and commercialization of bakery and pastry products, followed shortly by milling activities. At that time, in 1994, the company rented a shop and a small bakery within the same premises. These activities were transferred in 2008 to another company within the group, Fabrica de Pâine Șerban SRL (FPS), which has since developed the business. Currently, it owns a bakery in Filipești, Bacău County, serving its five own stores, a confectionery/pastry laboratory, and a fleet that serves neighboring localities, with distribution routes in Bacău and Vrancea counties, as well as national distribution through major retail platforms and directly (e.g. Lidl, Profi Rom Food, Rewe, Kaufland, Carrefour, Auchan, Cora, Mega Image, Supeco). activity of Interagroaliment S.R.L. is the cultivation and commercialization of cereals, oilseeds, and

In 2004, INTERAGROALIMENT SRL (IAA) acquired a broiler chicken farm in Zorleni commune, Vaslui County, and later purchased two other similar farms in Bacău County. Currently, the main vegetables, while the poultry farming and compound feed production business line has been transferred to Ferma Avicolă Șerban S.R.L.

In 2017, the poultry farming and meat commercialization activities were taken over by Ferma Avicolă Șerban S.R.L. (FAS), founded in 2008, with a total capacity of 880,000 heads per cycle (across 5 farms in 3 locations). Additionally, to increase the level of production integration, the company operates a compound feed production facility with a capacity of 7 tons per hour of finished product, using raw materials from the group's cereal production.

Since 2022, Grup Șerban has also owned an Angus cattle farm, consisting of 450 Angus cattle and 135 hectares of pasture for free-range cattle farming. The company aims to reach a herd size of 500 cattle and create the conditions for irrigated and overseeded pasture. On this land, the company

<!-- image -->

practices rotational grazing with a high level of technicality, leading to notable results by using techniques known as cover cropping. Increasing green mass production and improving natural pastures for rotational grazing involve the constant care and maintenance of the pasture areas.

Another company within the group is Șerban Distribuție S.R.L. (SD), founded in 2005. It serves as a support company for the group, acting as the vehicle through which services (such as transport and agricultural works) are provided to the group's companies. Additionally, it handles the procurement of agricultural inputs and has the technical and material infrastructure necessary for carrying out these activities.

In addition to the three companies briefly described above (FPS, FAS, and SD), the parent company INTERAGROALIMENT S.R.L. focused on cultivating agricultural land in Bacău, Vaslui, and Vrancea counties until 2018, when activities began to consolidate under GSH (Grup Șerban Holding S.A.). This activity started in 2005 with the cultivation of 1,500 hectares. Currently, Interagroaliment S.R.L. manages approximately 10,000 hectares in the Bacău, Vaslui, and Vrancea counties, organized into five crop farms, stretching from the Carpathians to the Prut River in the southern part of the North-East Region. It also owns grain and oilseed storage facilities with a capacity of over 162,000 tons across 10 locations in Bacău and Vaslui counties. As the largest company in the Moldova region, it operates under CAEN code 150 - Mixed farming activities (crop cultivation). At the GSH level, more than 12,000 hectares of arable land and pastures are farmed, with over 10,000 hectares managed by IAA, and the rest cultivated through affiliated companies.

GSH was founded in 2018 with the purpose of consolidating the activities of the other existing companies in the group. Thus, the consolidation of activities, including financial statements, began in 2019, prior to which the consolidation had been carried out through Interagroaliment SRL. GSH is both the parent company according to OMF no. 1802/2014 and the declaring entity for the fiscal group formed for corporate income tax purposes, starting with the declaration year 2022. It benefits from the advantages provided by legislation regarding holding companies and fiscal consolidation.

On December 8, 2021, Grup Șerban Holding conducted a private placement of shares, in which 80 investors participated, including 18 qualified investors and 62 retail investors. The company issued 5,974,247 shares as part of a capital increase operation, with an individual nominal value of 1 leu, at a price of 2.12 lei per share, raising 12.7 million lei from investors. On February 28, 2022, the company's shares were admitted for trading on the SMT segment of the Bucharest Stock Exchange under the symbol GSH, with a market capitalization of 359 million lei.

## EXPERIENCE

Given the founding dates of the Issuer's companies, as well as its collaboration with numerous clients and suppliers across various sectors aligned with GSH's business lines, the Issuer's experience is extensive. The most relevant aspects in this regard are:

- The increase in the areas farmed by the Issuer to over 10,000 hectares.

- The transition process towards no-tillage farming for an area of 7,000 hectares using no-till technology. The application of such technology leads to a significant increase in EBITDA by year 5, driven by a substantial reduction in fertilizer costs. As a result, the Group anticipates that the revenues generated through the use of this technology will remain stable, as no-till practices produce consistent yields compared to the traditional model that involves plowing the soil.

- The application of organic fertilizer, with the company successfully applying over 100,000 tons of compost derived from the waste produced at its poultry farms.

The establishment of partnerships with over 2,000 farmers from the Moldova region, from whom the Issuer purchases agricultural products that are subsequently marketed.

- The development of a storage capacity for grains and oilseeds exceeding 162,000 tons.

- The development of a proprietary brand for marketing the chicken meat produced by FAS.

- The development of proprietary brands for the commercialization of bakery products (Granero) and confectionery products (LuMa creator de gust).

- The development of a bakery, a confectionery laboratory, and five stores under the Băcănia Șerban brand, as well as the LuMa cafeteria. Currently, the total production capacity for bakery and pastry products is 20 tons per 24 hours, with collaborations established with strong brands such as Selgros, Mega Image, Kaufland, Carrefour, Penny, Profi, Cora, Lidl, and Auchan.

The development of a state-of-the-art agricultural logistics park, comprising over 250 vehicles and agricultural machinery, along with associated equipment.

- The development of an irrigation system covering 1,200 hectares in 2022, with plans to expand to 1,600 hectares in 2023, and a potential to reach 3,000 hectares. In 2022, of the 1,200 irrigated hectares, 300 hectares were irrigated using drip irrigation, with 600 hectares planned to be irrigated by drip in 2023.

## DIFFERENTIATION FROM COMPETITION

## IMPACT ON THE ENVIRONMENT

Agriculture is often perceived as a sector with a negative impact on the environment, primarily due to the widespread use of pesticides. The impact of pesticides is largely related to their effects on non-target species. Pesticides are chemical preparations used to kill fungal or animal pests. It is estimated that 98% of sprayed insecticides and 95% of herbicides reach other areas, as they are dispersed or spread across entire agricultural fields. Runoff can transport pesticides into aquatic environments, while wind can carry them to other fields, pastures, human settlements, and undeveloped areas, posing a risk to other species. Over time, repeated application increases pest resistance, and the effects on other species can facilitate the re-emergence of pests.

The Issuer uses only approved, high-quality pesticides that have been proven to have minimal environmental impact. The negative impact of pesticides on the environment can be offset by the use of organic fertilizers-a practice employed by the Issuer not only for certified organic crops but also for conventional ones.

Additionally, the Issuer utilizes renewable energy through photovoltaic power plants with an installed capacity of over 400KWp, consisting of more than 1,000 solar panels , covering over 2,000 square meters of rooftops converted into solar power plants for the poultry business line.

There are no ongoing or anticipated environmental protection-related disputes.

## DIVIDEND POLICY

The Board of Directors of Grup Șerban Holding S.A. declares that the company is committed to adhering to the Corporate Governance Principles applicable to issuers whose securities are traded on the Multilateral Trading System operated by the Bucharest Stock Exchange S.A. as a regulated market operator.

In line with these principles, the Issuer commits to adopting a dividend policy, which outlines the guidelines for distributing net profits, and declares that it will adhere to this policy. Consequently, the Board of Directors of the Issuer sets forth the following relevant principles regarding its dividend policy:

- The Issuer recognizes the shareholders' right to be remunerated in the form of dividends as a way to participate in the net operating profits, as well as to reward the capital invested in the company.
- As a company with significant development potential, the management of the Issuer aims to strike a balance between rewarding shareholders and maintaining access to capital needed for growth. Therefore, the Issuer proposes a hybrid dividend policy model, which includes both the distribution of free shares and cash dividends.
- The Issuer proposes to reward its shareholders, employees, and collaborators by granting free shares following the capitalization of a portion of the accumulated net profits. Under this model, the capital will remain with the Issuer and be reinvested in activities and expenses aimed at increasing the business value, while investors will be rewarded for their contribution with free shares. The proposal for dividend distribution, in the form of free shares, including the distribution rate, will be made by the Issuer's Board of Directors through financial announcements to investors.

Any changes to the company's dividend policy will be communicated to investors in a timely manner. Following the admission of the Issuer to trading on the SMT market of the Bucharest Stock Exchange, a formal dividend policy will be published on the Company's official website, www.grup-serban.ro, under the Investors section.

To date, Grup Șerban Holding S.A. has not distributed or paid dividends to shareholders in previous years.

## Outlook on the Issuer's Activity

## DEVELOPMENT DIRECTIONS

As the Issuer, through the group to which it belongs, operates in several fields of activity, future plans depend on each company and the specific activities they carry out.

## Investment projects in irrigation systems

Future projects in plant production focus on both vegetable growing and large-scale crops. The vegetable growing project is being developed on a 3,000-hectares irrigation system in the Siret Meadow.

Currently, within this system, the Issuer irrigates 1,200 hectares and estimates that by 2023 it will irrigate 1,600 hectares, with the goal of reaching the full 3,000 hectares by the end of the year. The Issuer plans to develop vegetables in rotation, with a stable crop plan that includes potatoes, other root vegetables, cereals, and technical plants. This will result in a production capacity of 550 hectares in the root crops sector as a first step by the end of the 2022-2023 agricultural year.

In 2023, the estimated production in the root sector is 25,000 tons , with the potential to reach 50,000 tons.

<!-- image -->

## Development of regenerative agriculture practices

No-till technology (without soil disturbance through processing) is a fundamental component of sustainable agriculture and is particularly well-suited for large farms. This technology involves minimal tillage using specialized seed drills.

## No-till technology offers the following advantages:

- Reduces diesel costs and working time by up to 50%
- Increases production by up to 10% after land conversion, with a long-term cumulative effect
- Preserves soil fertility, aiding in its regeneration
- Increases organic matter in the soil, enhancing overall soil health

Conserve water from the sol.

<!-- image -->

<!-- image -->

For the 2022-2023 agricultural year, the Issuer plans to convert an area of 7,500 hectares to the no-till system (without soil disturbance through processing), with a defined crop rotation plan. In the medium term, this will allow the Issuer to increase the large crop areas without plowing, with reduced chemical fertilizer costs, improved soil quality, increased operating revenues, and reduced operating expenses, positively impacting EBITDA growth.

## Development of storage capacities

Currently, the Issuer has a total storage capacity for cereals, oilseeds, and vegetables of over 162,000 tons. Of this, 6,500 tons represent the functional capacity for vegetable storage, under controlled temperature, humidity, and ventilation conditions, with the necessary logistics to keep them in optimal conditions for up to 6 months.

## Ongoing projects for storage capacity:

As part of its investment plan, the Issuer aims to develop a cold storage facility (with controlled temperature, ventilation, and humidity) for bulk and boxed vegetables, with a storage capacity of up to 15,000 tons by 2023. GSH estimates that 70-80% of the vegetable production capacity will be directed towards industrialization, benefiting from economies of scale. This will position the company as a long-term partner for international processors, contributing to shortening the supply chain by focusing on production, conditioning, storage, and timely delivery, thereby adding value.

## RISKS

Reputation risk - This risk is inherent to the Issuer's activity, as clients may be dissatisfied due to the outcome of evaluations rather than the quality of the evaluation reports. To address this, the Company plans to train customer relations personnel to improve communication (customer care).

Employee risk - The limited availability of qualified candidates for evaluations during the recruitment process poses a risk to the company.

Competition risk Changes in strategy by existing competitors or the entry of new competitors, especially from outside Romania with significant budgets for aggressive growth, could intensify competition and pressure the company's operations, potentially leading to a decrease in turnover or profit.

Risk associated with key personnel - The company's activities require advanced knowledge and specialization. The company relies on recruiting and retaining management staff and skilled employees. Medium and long-term profitability depends on the performance of qualified employees, staff, and executive management, who are crucial to the company's development.

Risk associated with financial forecasts - Financial forecasts are based on fulfilling the business development plan. Currently, there is no formal forecasting policy within the company. The company monitors budget performance monthly, and makes quarterly adjustments if necessary. Although the budget is prepared prudently, there is a risk of non-fulfillment, meaning reported data may differ significantly from forecasted estimates due to unforeseen factors.

Interest rate and financing sources risk - If the economic environment deteriorates, the Issuer may be unable to secure new loans under previously favorable conditions, leading to increased financing costs and negatively impacting the company's financial situation.

Price risk - This risk refers to the possibility that market prices for the products and services sold by the company may fluctuate, rendering existing contracts unprofitable. The company carefully monitors market prices and can withdraw from contracts if they become unfavorable.

Personal data protection risk - The company collects, stores, and uses data protected by personal data protection laws. Although the Issuer takes precautions in line with privacy laws, particularly under the General

<!-- image -->

Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/79 and Romania's regulations (effective from May 25, 2018), the risk of data leaks cannot be entirely eliminated.

Seasonality risk Most of the income from crop sales occurs in the second half of the year (summer-autumn) unless the company decides to store crops for later sale. The company believes this risk is mitigated by its 167,000-ton storage capacity for cereals and plants.

Risk related to significant customers - There is a risk that unfavorable circumstances could prevent the Company from renewing contracts with certain major customers, which could have a material negative effect on its business, financial condition, or operating results.

Risk of garnishment of the Issuer's accounts - Garnishment is a forced execution measure that may result in the Issuer's accounts being blocked if creditors request it to recover claims. This could cause difficulties or make it impossible for the company to meet obligations under agreed terms.

Risk associated with the business development plan - The company has a moderate development plan that can be adjusted according to available resources if necessary. However, economic crises or new market competitors may reduce sales revenues. While the company expects growth based on its development plan, unforeseen events could negatively impact its financial condition.

Risk associated with agricultural activities - The company's business plans are based on a model utilizing both owned and leased land, with an average lease contract duration of 8.5 years. There is a risk that contracts may not be renewed after expiration.

Risk associated with APIA subsidies - The company benefits from APIA subsidies, which are incorporated into annual budgets under the Common Agricultural Policy. These subsidies may change year to year, though the extent of the changes cannot be estimated in advance.

Liquidity risk - This includes the risk of receivables not being recovered. Due to the nature of its business, the company maintains a level of receivables and debts that enables optimal operations.

Counterparty risk - This risk arises when a third party or legal entity fails to meet its obligations, leading to financial loss. The company is exposed to credit risk from its operations (mainly foreign trade receivables) and financial activities (such as bank deposits, foreign exchange transactions, and other financial instruments).

<!-- image -->

General economic risks - The Issuer's activities are closely tied to the financial and banking sector. In a recession, lending activity may decline, reducing valuation requests for new financing. However, the Issuer expects an increase in requests due to insolvencies and foreclosures.

Tax and legal risk - The Issuer is governed by Romanian law, which, although harmonized with EU law, is subject to amendments or the introduction of new laws that could impact the company's activities. Romanian law can be unclear, subject to varying interpretations, and frequently changes. Amendments to tax and legal legislation, or events arising from their application, may result in fines or lawsuits that could impact the company.

Litigation risk - Despite its efforts to comply with legal provisions, the Issuer faces risks related to litigation and legal proceedings. Contractual and non-contractual claims from counterparties, customers, competitors, or regulatory authorities could negatively impact the company's reputation.

Cash-flow risk This is the risk that the company may not meet its payment obligations when due. Prudent cash-flow management involves maintaining adequate cash levels and financial availability through credit facilities. The company monitors expected cash inflows from receivables and cash outflows for trade and other debts, maintaining a healthy cash flow through its business model, which includes monitoring services for a fixed monthly fee.

Political and military instability in the region - Political and military instability, such as the war in Ukraine, could lead to unfavorable economic conditions, social unrest, or, in extreme cases, military conflict. This situation may result in decreased investment, currency fluctuations, interest rate hikes, reduced credit availability, trade and capital flow disruptions, and rising energy prices, all of which could adversely affect the Issuer's business and financial position.

Other risks The outlined risks are considered the most significant known risks at the time of writing. However, there may be additional risks unknown to the Issuer or not foreseen, which could impact the company's actual results, financial conditions, and share price. Investors should conduct due diligence to assess the investment opportunity.

- a) erau disponibile la momentul la care situatiile financiare pentru acele perioade au fost aprobate spre a fi emise;

Corectarea erorilor semnificative aferente exercitiilor financiare precedente se efectueazà pe seama rezultatului reportat. Erorile nesemnificative aferente exercitiilor financiare precedente se corecteazš, de asemenea, pe seama rezultatului reportat. Totusi, erorile nesemnificative fi corectate pe seama contului de profit si pierdere. pot
